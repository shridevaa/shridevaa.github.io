About me

I’ve been a little obsessed with videos for a long time. I love watching them, but even more than that, I love figuring out how they are made and trying to make my own.

I started making short films in high school, which gradually got me interested in editing and storytelling. Since then, I’ve worked on YouTube content, long form videos, promotional campaigns, and TV content while learning about pacing, audience retention, and what keeps people watching.

Being a Computer Science Engineer, I’ve always had a technical side as well. AI has made the combination of technology and creativity even more interesting for me. I enjoy experimenting with different AI models, building creative workflows, and seeing how far an idea can be taken, from a simple concept to visuals, video, sound, and the final edit.

What makes my work different is the combination of filmmaking, editing, technology, and the curiosity to keep trying new things.
